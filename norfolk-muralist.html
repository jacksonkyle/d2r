Norfolk Muralist

School, museum, and public-space murals across Norfolk, VA

Matthew Jackson has been painting murals for Norfolk schools, museums, and public spaces for over 20 years. Based 25 minutes away in Virginia Beach, he's available for on-site consultation across Norfolk with no out-of-area surcharge — and his portfolio in the city includes one of its more visible cultural installations: the murals at the Norfolk Children's Museum.

Norfolk's mix of historic architecture, naval heritage, and growing arts scene makes it a particularly rewarding place to paint. Matthew has worked with public-school districts in the city and on private commercial commissions, scaling from interior accent walls to multi-room installations.

Featured Norfolk projects

  • Norfolk Children's Museum — multi-room mural installation; one of the museum's signature visual experiences for visiting families and schools
  • Norfolk public-school murals — mascots, library walls, gymnasium and hallway pieces
  • Commercial and residential commissions across the city

Mural services in Norfolk

  • School murals — mascots, educational themes, library walls, gym walls, planetarium ceilings
  • Museum, gallery, and public-space installations
  • Commercial murals — restaurant interiors, retail spaces, corporate lobbies
  • Residential — accent walls, kids' rooms, basements, garages
  • Exterior building murals with weather-resistant finishes
  • Sculpture and dimensional surface painting

How a Norfolk project works

Send photos of the surface, rough dimensions, and a sentence about what you'd like painted — Matthew can usually drive over for an in-person walk-through within a few days, or work from photos if a remote quote is preferred. Concept sketches arrive 1-2 weeks after an accepted quote, and most interior murals are painted in 3-7 days on site.

Need a Norfolk muralist? Send a quote request with a photo of your space, or call 757-450-2884. Replies typically within 1-2 business days.
