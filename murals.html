Custom Murals

Hand-painted murals for schools, businesses, and homes

Matthew Jackson has been painting custom murals professionally since 2002. Working primarily out of Virginia Beach, his murals appear in schools across Norfolk, Suffolk, Chesapeake, and Virginia Beach, as well as in businesses and private residences across the United States.

Every mural is hand-painted on site. Surfaces include cinder block, drywall, brick, wood, metal, and exterior building façades. Subjects range from school mascots and educational themes to ocean scenes, historical scenes, sports imagery, and abstract designs developed in collaboration with each client.

Typical mural projects include:

  • School murals — mascots, educational themes, library walls, gym walls, planetarium ceilings
  • Business murals — exterior building murals, indoor branding murals, dimensional logos
  • Residential murals — children's bedrooms, basements, garages, accent walls, ceilings
  • Public installations — community centers, museums, fire stations, recreation centers

How quoting works: share photos of the surface, rough dimensions, the subject you have in mind, and your timeline. Matthew will reply with a fixed price and a sketch within a few business days. Request a quote or call 757-450-2884.
