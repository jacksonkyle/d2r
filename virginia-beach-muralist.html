Virginia Beach Muralist

Local. Professional. Hand-painted since 2002.

Matthew Jackson is a Virginia Beach-born professional artist who's been painting custom murals here, and across the Hampton Roads region, since 2002. His murals appear in Virginia Beach public schools, the Oceanfront, Sandbridge, Landstown, and at private residences and businesses across the city.

Working locally means there's no travel surcharge for projects in Virginia Beach — Matthew can walk a site, get accurate measurements, and start the design process the same week. Most VB clients have a quote and concept sketch in hand within 5-7 days of first contact.

Mural services in Virginia Beach

Matthew handles the full range of mural work for VB clients:

  • Public-school murals — mascots, library walls, gymnasiums, planetariums
  • Business murals — Oceanfront restaurants and shops, retail interiors, corporate lobbies
  • Residential murals — beach houses, kids' rooms, garages, accent walls
  • Exterior building murals — UV-resistant outdoor work that holds up to coastal weather
  • Floor and ceiling murals — including planetarium-style ceiling work
  • Live mural events — public-facing art installations as a community draw

Why hire local

For a Virginia Beach mural project, hiring local matters: site visits are free, no out-of-state travel costs are baked into the quote, and Matthew is available for follow-up touch-ups years after the original install. The coastal climate also matters — VB exteriors take a beating from salt air and humidity, and mural work here uses different primer and topcoat systems than inland projects.
