Creative Renovations

Artistic home renovations across Hampton Roads

Beyond mural work, Matthew Jackson takes on full creative renovation projects: bathrooms, kitchens, cabinets, fireplaces, and outdoor spaces. Every project is approached as an artwork first and a renovation second — the goal is a finished space that's genuinely one-of-a-kind, not a copy of a showroom.

Renovation work draws on the same toolkit as the mural practice — color, composition, surface treatment, and hand-applied finishes — combined with the construction craft needed to deliver a durable, code-compliant result. Matthew handles design, sourcing, and installation, and works directly with homeowners from concept through completion.

Renovation services include:

  • Custom bathroom renovations — tile work, custom vanities, painted accent walls
  • Custom kitchen renovations — cabinetry refinishing, painted backsplashes, statement walls
  • Custom fireplace renovations — stone, tile, and painted surrounds
  • Custom cabinets and built-ins
  • Outdoor projects — fences, decks, garden walls with painted accents

Project consultation: Matthew will visit on-site in the Hampton Roads area to evaluate the space and discuss design direction. Out-of-area projects are quoted from photos and measurements. Request a quote or call 757-450-2884.
